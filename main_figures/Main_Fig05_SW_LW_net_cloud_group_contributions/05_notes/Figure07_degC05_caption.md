Figure 7. Cloud-type-resolved daytime shortwave (SW), longwave (LW), and net total-contribution pathways across the tropical Pacific subregions using the +/-0.5 C Nino3.4 ENSO definition. Panels (a)-(c) present El Nino minus La Nina group-total contributions for SW, LW, and net cloud radiative effects (CRE), respectively, across the western Pacific (WP), central Pacific (CP), and eastern Pacific (EP). Rows identify the five physical cloud groups used in the pathway analysis. Values are derived from the contribution-consistent daytime candidate chain, and black dots indicate group-component contributions whose 95% moving-block-bootstrap confidence intervals exclude zero. The pathways are interpreted as cloud-type-resolved diagnostics and not as an exact reconstruction of the direct all-sky CRE response.
